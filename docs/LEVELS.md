# Snake Game - 30-Level Campaign Overview

This document contains the ASCII layout map and hazard statistics for all 30 levels across the 6 campaign tiers.

- **Board Dimensions:** 15 columns $\times$ 20 rows (300 cells total).
- **Starting Snake:** Segments at `[7, 9]` (head), `[7, 10]`, `[7, 11]` facing UP.
- **Starting Safe Zone:** Columns 6–8, Rows 4–12 (always clear of hazards).
- **Target Score:** 100 points per level to clear and unlock the next level.

## Tier 4: Blink Canyon (Levels 16–20)
*Focus: Timed blinking spike fields (OFF -> WARN -> ON).*

### Level 16: First Blink
- **Mechanic Hint:** "Spikes blink — safe when faint, fatal when ON!"
- **Hazards:** 6 Spikes (Group 1), 12 Rocks (282 free cells)

### Level 17: Pulse Lanes
- **Hazards:** 16 Spikes (Groups 1 & 2 in counter-phase), 8 Rocks (276 free cells)

### Level 18: Spike & Stone
- **Hazards:** 2 Portals, 8 Spikes, 4 Rocks (286 free cells)

### Level 19: Rhythm Bridge
- **Hazards:** 12 Spikes (Groups 1 & 2), 12 Rocks (276 free cells)

### Level 20: The Blink Canyon
- **Type:** Boss 👑
- **Hazards:** 8 Spikes (Groups 1 & 2 alternating), 16 Rocks (276 free cells)
